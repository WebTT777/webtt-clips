Privacy Policy for WebTT_Clips

Last updated: November 11, 2026

WebTT_Clips ("the App") is an internal tool that lets our team schedule and publish video content to TikTok accounts owned by our organization. This policy describes the data the App actually processes.

Data we collect

  • Telegram data of team members: Telegram user ID, username and interface language, used to identify users and send notifications.
  • TikTok account data: when an account owner authorizes the App through TikTok, we receive the account identifier, display name, avatar and access and refresh tokens. We also read profile data, post data and performance metrics (such as views and likes) of the authorized accounts.
  • Content you submit: videos, covers, captions, hashtags, privacy and publishing settings, and the publishing schedule.
  • Activity log: records of important actions in the App (who did what and when), visible to the App owner.

How we use data

We use this data only to authenticate users, publish the videos you choose to your TikTok accounts, show account statistics, send notifications and keep the App secure.

Sharing

We send your videos, captions and settings to TikTok to publish them. We use infrastructure and hosting providers that process data on our behalf to run the App. We do not sell or rent your data and do not share it with any other third parties.

Retention and deletion

  • Video files are deleted from our servers after publishing; files of failed posts are deleted within 7 days.
  • Access tokens are stored encrypted and are deleted when an account is disconnected.
  • Captions, schedules, statistics and the activity log are kept while the account is in use.
  • You can revoke access at any time in your TikTok settings (Security, Manage app permissions), or request deletion of your data by emailing selleknatalie@gmail.com. We will delete it within 30 days.

Security

Tokens are encrypted, access to the App is limited to invited users, and secrets are stored outside of the source code.
