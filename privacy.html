Privacy Policy for WebTT_Clips

Last updated: November 10, 2026

WebTT_Clips ("the App") is a tool that lets users schedule and publish their own video content to their TikTok account.

Data we access

When you connect your TikTok account via Login Kit, we receive your TikTok open ID, display name, avatar and an access token. We use them only to identify your account and to publish the videos you choose, with the captions you provide.

Data we store

We store your access token, the videos and captions you submit, and the publishing schedule. We do not sell, rent or share your data with third parties.

Retention and deletion

Data is kept only as long as needed to provide the service. You can revoke access at any time in your TikTok settings (Security, Manage app permissions), or request deletion of all your data by emailing YOUR_EMAIL. We will delete it within 30 days.

Security

Tokens and content are stored on protected servers with restricted access.
