WebTT_Clips

WebTT_Clips is an internal publishing tool for our team. It helps us prepare, schedule and publish our own short videos to the TikTok accounts owned by our organization, and manage them through TikTok's official APIs.

What it does

  • Team members upload videos and prepare captions and hashtags.
  • Posts are scheduled per account and published to TikTok accounts that the team owns, after the account owner authorizes the app through TikTok.
  • AI-generated content is labeled as AI-generated.
  • Performance metrics of our own accounts are shown to our team.

Access is limited to invited members of our team.
